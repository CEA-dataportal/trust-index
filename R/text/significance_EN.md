**Significance tests**

To assess whether differences between groups are statistically significant, we use t-tests to compare the mean scores for competency and value questions across groups. For the questions analysed, the observed differences are not statistically significant, which may partly reflect the small sample sizes within some groups.

The table indicates whether the results for beneficiaries, volunteers, and others are significantly different from one another. We use a 95% confidence level and adjust the p-values to account for multiple comparisons.
